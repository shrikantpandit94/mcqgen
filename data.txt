What is Drawing Assist?
What is Smart select and Sketch to image?

Drawing assist is a new feature that transforms your rough sketches into beautiful illustrations through Galaxy AI. With the <Z Flip6>, this tool can be used in various scenarios and has three access points. 

First, when you tap Smart Select from the Edge panel and select an image area, "Sketch to image" button will appear. This lets you draw directly over the selected image. 
Second, when you draw a simple drawing, "Sketch to image" menu will become activate in Note Assist menu. Then you can trace around the drawing you want to transform into an illustration. 
Thrid, you can add drawings directly onto your photos. Select a photo in Gallery, go to the Photo assist menu at the bottom, and tap  "Sketch to image" button. 

With Smart select, you can quickly select content on any screen to view AI actions. Anything on the screen can be selected to continue the Galaxy AI experience, so just open Edge panel from the side of the screen and tap Smart select to tap or draw on the image or text to select. One of the AI actions is called Sketch to Image. When you select the image, the AI action bar pops up with the Sketch to Image feature in it. Tap Sketch to Image from the bar to start your sketch on the image; then, if you tap Generate, you can get a totally new image. The Galaxy AI composes a new image based on your sketches with the original image.
So, if you cannot find the exact photo you want, just create it easily with your drawings!
* Samsung account login required.
